Shoulder pain is a common condition that can be caused by several factors. Whether it's from overuse, repetitive motion, or an injury, shoulder pain can be debilitating. The pain may also happen when you are involved in an accident, especially if you need to use your shoulders to brace for impact.

Here are the common causes of shoulder pain:

**Shoulder Dislocation**

Shoulder dislocation is a type of injury that occurs when the head of the upper arm bone moves out of the shoulder socket. This can occur in several ways, including a fall or trauma to the shoulder, a strong blow, or a sports injury.

**Shoulder Separation**

A shoulder separation occurs when the soft tissue between the collarbone and shoulder blades is torn or damaged.

**Shoulder Fracture**

A shoulder fracture is a break of the bone which encases the shoulder joint and allows movement of the arm and shoulder. A fracture may be due to a fall, collision, or a sudden twist of the shoulder.

**Shoulder Arthritis**

Shoulder arthritis is a relatively common condition in which the cartilage that covers the bones in the shoulder joint wears away. The bones rub against each other causing pain, loss of motion, and decreased strength in the shoulder.

Shoulder pain is not something to take lightly. It is important to seek medical attention from your doctor or physical therapist as soon as possible. Remember, your shoulders are used for almost everything you do in your day-to-day life.
