## What Causes Scoliosis Pain?

Scoliosis pain can arise from various sources within the scoliotic spine. Misalignments in the spine and abnormal curvatures put additional stress on surrounding muscles, leading to muscle spasms, tension, and soreness. Compressed or damaged spinal discs can further contribute to pain, radiating to the back, neck, and even down the arms and legs. In severe cases, nerve compression may occur, causing radiating pain, numbness, and tingling.

While not all scoliosis cases exhibit noticeable symptoms, pain is a common indicator of the condition. Early detection and intervention are crucial to managing pain effectively and preventing scoliosis progression.
