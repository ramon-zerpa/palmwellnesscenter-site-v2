Massage has been used for thousands of years as a natural way to relieve pain, reduce stress, and promote overall wellbeing. While massages are often associated with indulgence and general relaxation, they can also serve as legitimate medical treatment when performed by a licensed massage therapist.

In this blog post, we’ll dig into what is medical massage, how it differs from traditional massage, what conditions it can treat, and what you can expect from a therapeutic massage session. We’ll also explore some of the proven benefits this type of specialized massage can provide patients.

## Massages aren’t just for “spa days.”

While massages can provide relaxation and be part of spa days, they can also serve as an effective medical treatment for various health conditions. Medical massage, also known as therapeutic massage, goes beyond the typical spa massage to alleviate pain, promote healing, and improve overall well-being.

The techniques used in medical massage therapy, such as trigger point therapy, myofascial release, and neuromuscular therapy, are specifically designed to address issues like muscle pain, restricted mobility, injuries, and nerve pain.

## The Benefits of Medical Massage

Medical massage provided by licensed medical massage therapists can provide real pain relief and be part of treatment plans for many chronic conditions. The techniques used in medical massage therapy can help manage pain, reduce inflammation, improve function, and activate the body's natural healing mechanisms.

## Can I Get a Medical Massage at a Spa?

While spas may offer relaxing Swedish massages, medical massage requires advanced training and licensure. Massage therapists must have specialized skills to effectively treat medical conditions. So while you can get a great traditional massage at a spa, see a licensed medical massage therapist if you are looking to alleviate chronic pain, heal injuries, or develop a targeted treatment plan.

## What Is the Difference Between a Traditional and Medical Massage?

The main difference is that medical massage is result-driven treatment focused on specific health conditions, while traditional massage is aimed more broadly at relaxation and overall wellness. ## What Kinds of Conditions Can a Medical Massage Therapy Treat?

Medical massage can be an effective part of treatment plans for conditions including back, neck, and shoulder pain, headaches and migraines, sports injuries, TMJ dysfunction, osteoarthritis, carpal tunnel syndrome, and more. For many chronic conditions, massage therapy is recommended in conjunction with other medical treatment.

## What Are the Benefits of a Medical Massage?

The benefits of medical massage include pain relief, reduced muscle tension, improved circulation and range of motion, faster healing for injuries, reduced inflammation, relief from headaches, restored nerve functioning, lowered stress and anxiety, and improved overall well-being. Medical massage helps activate the body’s natural healing and pain relief mechanisms.

## What Are Some of the Medical Massage Techniques?

Some common techniques used in medical massage include trigger point therapy to release tight muscles and knots; myofascial release to free restrictions in connective tissue; neuromuscular therapy to reduce pain and spasms; positional release to reduce muscle tension; and manual lymphatic drainage to improve circulation and drainage. The medical massage therapist will utilize the techniques suitable to the patient’s condition.

## Why You Should Try Medical or Therapeutic Massage

Medical massage, also known as therapeutic massage, can provide many benefits beyond relaxation and stress relief. If you are suffering from chronic pain or restricted mobility, medical massage may be able to help.

### It can ease discomfort and pain.

Medical massage utilizes techniques like trigger point therapy, neuromuscular therapy, myofascial release, and deep tissue massage to address issues like muscle pain, joint pain, restricted range of motion, injuries, and chronic conditions.

The massage therapist targets affected muscles, connective tissue, and joints to reduce inflammation, release tension, improve circulation, and activate the body's natural pain relief and healing mechanisms.

### What to expect at a massage therapy visit?

Your first appointment will likely involve an assessment of your medical history and condition. The massage therapist will then tailor a therapeutic massage treatment plan to target your specific issues.

Techniques may include trigger point therapy to release knotted muscles, myofascial release to free restrictions in connective tissue, and positional release to gently coax muscles into a more relaxed state. Expect to feel some residual soreness after your first few sessions as your body adjusts.

### How therapeutic massage is different from relaxation massage.

Relaxation massage found at spas focuses on overall circulation and inducing a general state of calm. Therapeutic massage targets specific problem areas and medical conditions like chronic back pain, headaches, injuries, restricted mobility, etc.

It aims to provide real, lasting pain relief and improved functioning. Therapeutic massage uses advanced techniques and training to treat medical issues, not just temporary relaxation.
