## What is Whiplash?

Whiplash is a neck injury caused by a sudden collision or jerking motion, often occurring during car accidents when a vehicle is rear-ended. The forceful hyperextension and hyperflexion of the neck can strain or tear soft tissues like muscles, ligaments, and the intervertebral disc, leading to pain, neck stiffness, and inflammation. Patients may also experience headaches, nerve irritation, spasms, and reduced range of motion. Without proper health care and treatment, whiplash can cause long-term issues affecting the nervous system, joint flexibility, and overall quality of life.

### Signs and Symptoms of Whiplash

Whiplash can present a range of symptoms, often appearing within 24 hours of an accident. Common signs include:

- **Neck stiffness and pain**, especially with movement.
- **Dizziness and headaches**, often radiating from the base of the skull.
- **Shoulder and upper back pain**, sometimes accompanied by muscle tension.
- **Blurred vision**, **tinnitus**, or a feeling of vertigo.
- **Fatigue**, **insomnia**, and **difficulty concentrating**.

For Tampa residents with active and demanding lifestyles, ignoring these symptoms can lead to long-term complications, including nerve irritation, vertigo, and persistent neck stiffness, such as chronic pain, reduced range of motion, and inflammation surrounding the affected muscles.
