Chiropractors are not just limited to treating back and neck pain; chiropractic is an excellent solution for many kinds of soreness, injury, and different types of pain due to slip and fall incidents.

Slip and fall incidents are unique and require specialized attention. The injuries may not be the same as those commonly sustained when playing sports or involved in auto accidents.

## Consulting A Chiropractor After a Slip and Fall

Chiropractic treatments are proven to be effective for a multitude of issues that can arise from falls, including aching, tingling, and numbness in the arms or legs or other extremities, headaches, upper and lower back pain.

Problems with walking, balance, and coordination, and difficulty sleeping are most likely to take place after a slip and fall. All of these symptoms can prove to be debilitating, and it is important to receive treatment as soon as possible.

If you have found yourself in a situation like this, then you should consult with a professional chiropractor at your earliest convenience.

## Why You Should Get Immediate Treatment After Slip and Fall Injuries

One of the most common accidents is slip and fall. If you fall, whether you slip on the ground, the sidewalk, or trip over something, the most important thing to do is to get immediate medical attention. Slip and fall injuries can lead to serious health problems if not treated immediately.

Many people injure themselves in slip and fall accidents, but what many of these people don't know is how important it is for them to get the necessary treatment immediately after these accidents.

Following a slip and fall accident, even a seemingly small injury can lead to complications and further injuries. If you're not sure if you have been seriously injured in a slip and fall accident, it's a good idea to consult a healthcare professional for a thorough assessment.

## Common Slip and Fall Injuries

Some [slip and fall injuries](/types-of-slip-and-fall-injuries/) that chiropractors treat and that you should know about include sprains, strains, and fractures. More severe injuries include contusions, dislocations, internal bleeding, and brain injury.

But the most common injuries encountered are the following:

- Neck Injury. Common slip and fall accidents can cause a great deal of damage to the neck, back, and spine. With mild to moderate cases, a chiropractor can assess and treat your problems.
- Back Injury. A back injury is one of the most common injuries sustained from a fall. When a person suffers a back injury, it can have a major effect on body function. Aside from chronic pain, there is increased risk of developing other serious injuries or experiencing further weakness, severe pain, or even temporary and permanent disability.
- Joint Injury. If the impact of the injuries affects the joints, movement may become difficult or impossible. The affected area may swell and require extra care.
