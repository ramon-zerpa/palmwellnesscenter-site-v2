In this article, we will describe some of the amazing benefits you can get from routine chiropractic adjustments.

## What is a chiropractic adjustment?

Before we explore the benefits of chiropractic adjustment, you should understand what exactly chiropractic adjustment is.

Chiropractic manipulation is a form of hands-on spinal care performed by a trained professional chiropractor or a doctor of chiropractic. Also known as manual manipulation, this type of physical therapy uses gentle pressure to help achieve proper alignment of your spine.

## How does it work?

So, what does chiropractic treatment do to your body? Manual adjustments provide pain relief by aligning your spinal joints, thereby releasing tension in your vertebra and reducing strain on your muscles. A properly aligned spine also serves as the gateway to the rest of your body, which means that the rest of your body will also relax and experience a release when your chiropractor treats you through manipulative techniques. Other bones, muscles, cartilage, and connective tissues will function better, and your body's natural healing process will be able to function as intended.

## Benefits of chiropractic adjustment

Visiting a chiropractor has many health benefits. Let's have a look at a few of these:

### Controls blood pressure

If you have high blood pressure, you are likely taking blood pressure medications. If that is the case, you should give chiropractic adjustments treatment a try.

A study published by the Human Journal of Hypertension shows that chiropractic adjustment provided similar results to those achieved through blood pressure medications. The study also explains that the aftereffect of chiropractic adjustment treatment continues for six months.

### Reduces neck pain and lower back pain

Why should you consider chiropractic care before going for pain medication or surgeries? Numerous studies show that spinal manipulation helps to decrease acute and subacute neck pain more than pain medication. It is a non-invasive, hands-on natural healing alternative. With the rise in opioid addictions, doctors are increasingly recommending chiropractic adjustment as an alternative to pain medication.

### Helps treat scoliosis patients

Scoliosis is a spinal condition in which the spinal column is abnormally curved. This curvature makes e posture abnormal, reduces the range of motion, and causes pain and breathing difficulties.

Chiropractic adjustment can be a great option for scoliosis treatment, as it can improve symptoms and prevent the progression of the condition.

### Reduces Sciatica pain

If your sciatic nerve is pressured or damaged, the pain will spread out from your lower back down to your legs. You should treat this chronic pain as soon as possible or it will lead to over chronic pain or over-medicating.

To avoid expensive surgeries or long-term use of medication, see a chiropractor. A chiropractor can relieve pressure from the sciatic nerve and reduce the number of painful days the patient experiences.

### Reduces inflammation

Inflammation is one of the major reasons for recurring pain, tension, and joint issues in your body. It is also associated with severe diseases like heart disease and cancer.

Chiropractic adjustment reduces your body's inflammatory response, improving not just affected areas, but the holistic health of the patient.

### Relieves Headaches

Spinal issues and back pain cause muscle tension and chronic headaches, including cervicogenic headaches and migraine headaches.

A chiropractor can help with these types of headaches. Studies show that cervical manipulation and upper thoracic spine manipulation are very effective in treating headaches.

If you are experiencing too many headaches, chiropractic adjustments can be a better option for you.

Your nervous system governs your every bodily function. Your spine contains lots of nerves that connect your brain with the entire body. So, if there are misalignments in your spine, then it can hamper your nervous system and induce significant changes in your body function.

## How chiropractic helps neurological issues:

- Increase the blood flow in the brain and throughout the body.
- Increase the cerebral spinal fluid flow.
- Enhance the nervous system function.
- Enhance body performance.

If you have neurological conditions like epilepsy and sclerosis, chiropractic adjustments can provide significant relief.

### Improves athletic performance

Athletes commonly experience back pain, tension, and inflammation due to their daily physical activities and sports injuries. That's why professional athletes and sports teams always have chiropractors. Spine alignment helps to reduce pain, muscle tension, and inflammation, which actually helps athletes achieve top body shape. It also boosts the athletes' immune system which helps to enhance the athletes' performance. Chiropractic adjustments are also very effective in dealing with injury pain.

### Improves Vertigo

Vertigo is a common condition for people who have experienced a neck or head injury. Vertigo is a symptom that makes you feel that the environment around you is spinning or moving. If it is severe, it creates difficulties in doing everyday tasks and also creates difficulties with keeping your balance.

The chiropractic adjustment treatment is able to fix the misalignment of your body which is causing vertigo. It also allows you to target joints and vertebrae that do not move correctly. With this treatment, the vertigo sensation is reduced.

### Reduces osteoarthritis symptoms

Cartilage degeneration causes severe osteoarthritis pain in the joints when the ends of bones rub together. Chiropractic adjustments will help to align the bone joints and will reduce the rubbing of the bones, reducing joint pain.
