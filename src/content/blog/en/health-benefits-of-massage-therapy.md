Pain and stress are interrelated. People experience stress due to overworking, underlying health conditions, or long-term injuries. Conversely, being under pressure and stress can lead to pain build-up in different areas of your body. Massage therapy may be a relaxing experience, but it is much more than just pampering.  
  
Therapeutic massage addresses ailments related to your physical and mental well-being. The practice is known to address chronic pain, musculoskeletal disorders, and even [cancer-related stress](https://www.ucsfhealth.org/treatments/massage). It also helps manage [anxiety and depression](https://www.ucsfhealth.org/treatments/massage).  
  
Maximize the benefits of [massage therapy](/massage-therapy/) for a healthy mind and body.

## Benefits of Massage Therapy

- **Massage Therapy For Fatigue And Stress. **Too much fatigue lowers the energy in your body and prevents you from getting enough sleep, leading to an increase in your level of stress. With the help of therapeutic massage, fatigue and stress are minimized and treated to boost your energy. During massage therapy sessions, the body responds by releasing [endorphins](https://www.betterhealth.vic.gov.au/health/ConditionsAndTreatments/massage#benefits-of-massage), a natural chemical in the body responsible for making yourself feel good, which helps in the management of your body’s pain and stress.
- **Massage Therapy For Injuries. **Injured patients require immediate care and medical attention to improve their current situation. If you have suffered injuries from [car accidents](/car-accident-rehabilitation/), playing sports or [slip and fall](/slip-and-fall-injury-treatment/), [massage therapy](/physical-therapy/) is very helpful in the recovery and rehabilitation process. Professional therapists are trained to assess the patient’s condition and apply the correct massage techniques to the injured tissues, which helps improve mobility and maintain a healthy circulation for faster recuperation.
- **Massage Therapy For Anxiety And Depression. **The relaxation effect from receiving massage therapy reduces the symptoms of [depression and anxiety](/massage-therapy/). It is a conventional method of treatment for these conditions. Addressing the physical symptoms of depression and anxiety helps maintain a calm mind and body. Hence, therapeutic massage prevents sleeping disorders, relieves fatigue and stress, loosens muscle tension, and contributes to your overall mental wellness.
- **Massage Therapy For Chronic Pain. **Massage therapy is highly recommended for [pain management](https://www.painscale.com/article/use-these-types-of-massage-for-chronic-pain). It alleviates pain conditions ranging from simple muscle discomfort to severe pain caused by comorbid medical conditions. Through therapeutic massage, pain management becomes more effective since treatment includes other parts of the body affected by the origin of the pain.

The techniques used in [massage therapy](/massage-therapy/) sessions are performed by licensed physical therapists. That’s why patients suffering from different kinds of pain experience positive results after every session.
