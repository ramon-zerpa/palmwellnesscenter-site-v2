[Slip and fall accidents](/slip-and-fall-injury-treatment/) are a common cause of emergency visits, especially among older adults and workers in high-risk environments. These accidents can cause a wide range of injuries—from mild sprains and bruises to serious conditions like broken bones, torn ligaments, or even brain and spinal cord trauma.

Many victims experience pain in the knee, ankle, wrist, arm, hip, or neck, often involving soft tissue, nerve, or ligament damage. Some injuries may heal with rest and physical therapy, while others lead to long-term complications like chronic pain, reduced mobility, or emotional distress.

## What is a Slip and Fall Accident?

A slip and fall accident occurs when an individual loses balance due to unsafe or hazardous conditions—such as wet floors, uneven surfaces, poor lighting, or icy sidewalks—resulting in injuries that can range from minor bruises and sprains to fractured bones, head trauma, or spinal cord damage.

These accidents often happen in public or private properties like a grocery store, parking lot, restaurant, or nursing home, and are typically the result of poor maintenance, missing safety features (like handrails), or overlooked hazards. Medically, the priority is to assess the injury, manage pain, and develop a treatment plan to prevent mobility limitations and long-term health complications.

Legally, slip and fall accidents may fall under premises liability—a concept that holds property owners or landlords accountable under their duty of care to maintain a safe environment. If that duty is breached and negligence is established, a person may be entitled to seek compensation for medical costs and other related damages.
