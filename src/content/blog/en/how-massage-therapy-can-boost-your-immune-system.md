## How Massage Therapy Supports and Boosts the Immune System

Massage therapy offers a range of benefits that support and boost the immune system. Let's explore some of the key ways in which massage therapy promotes immune health.

### Stress Reduction and Immune System

Massage therapy is known for its ability to reduce stress and promote relaxation. Chronic stress can have a detrimental effect on the immune system, making us more susceptible to illnesses. Massage helps to alleviate stress by reducing the levels of stress hormones like cortisol while increasing the production of "feel-good" hormones like serotonin and dopamine. By reducing stress, massage therapy indirectly supports immune function.

### Immune Cell Activation and Massage Therapy

Studies have shown that massage therapy can stimulate the activity of immune cells, such t cells such as natural killer cells, and lymphocytes. These cells play a crucial role in identifying and destroying pathogens and abnormal cells in the body. By enhancing the activity of these immune cells, massage therapy can bolster the body's defense mechanisms and improve immune function.

### Inflammation Regulation and Immune System

Chronic inflammation can negatively impact the body and immune system and contribute to the development of various diseases. Massage therapy has been found to have anti-inflammatory effects by reducing the production of inflammatory substances and promoting the release of anti-inflammatory molecules. By regulating inflammation, massage therapy can support the body's heightened immune response and reduce the risk of inflammatory conditions.
