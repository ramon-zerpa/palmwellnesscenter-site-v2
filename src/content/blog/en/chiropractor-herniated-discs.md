## What is a Herniated Disc?

Imagine your spine as a stack of vertebrae cushioned by spongy discs. These discs act as shock absorbers, protecting your spine from everyday wear and tear. A [herniated disc](https://www.mayoclinic.org/diseases-conditions/herniated-disk/symptoms-causes/syc-20354095) occurs when the inner gel-like material (nucleus pulposus) pushes through the outer fibrous casing (annulus fibrosus). This bulge or herniation can put pressure on nearby spinal nerves, leading to:

- **Pain:** Radiating pain in the back, arms, or legs, depending on the disc location.
- **Numbness:** Tingling or loss of sensation in the affected area.
- **Muscle weakness:** Difficulty moving or using the affected muscles.

## Symptoms of Disc Herniation

While disc herniations or disc protrusion can be asymptomatic, various symptoms can signal a problem.

- **Sharp or burning pain** especially when bending, lifting, or twisting can be a sign of a slipped disc.
- **Weakness or numbness** in the extremities affected by the nerve compression.
- **Muscle spasms:** Involuntary muscle contractions in the back or surrounding areas.
- **Difficulty standing or sitting for extended periods:** Pain, especially low back pain, can often worsen with sustained positions.

**If you experience any of these symptoms, it's crucial to seek professional evaluation.** Early diagnosis and proper treatment can prevent further damage and lead to faster recovery.

## Chiropractic Treatment: How Does It Work?

While surgery is often the go-to solution for severe herniated disc cases, a multi-pronged approach involving chiropractic care can effectively manage symptoms, address pain, and promote healing for many patients without the constant use of pain medications. Here's a breakdown of how chiropractic treatment works:

### Physical Examination to Evaluate Your Current Condition

The first step in any chiropractic treatment plan is a thorough physical examination. This usually involves:

- **A detailed discussion of your medical history and current symptoms:** Be prepared to answer questions about your pain location, intensity, and any aggravating or relieving factors during your first visit.
- **Range of motion and flexibility tests:** The chiropractor will assess your ability to move and bend in different directions to identify any limitations caused by the herniated disc.
- **Neurological examinations:** These tests check for muscle weakness, reflexes, and sensory function to determine if the herniated disc is affecting any nerves.
- **Palpation:** The chiropractor will gently feel your cervical spine and surrounding muscles to identify areas of tenderness or tension.

### Chiropractor Creates a Customized Treatment Plan

Based on the findings of the physical examination, your chiropractor will develop a personalized treatment plan tailored to your specific needs and goals. This plan may include:

- **Spinal manipulation:** Gentle adjustments to realign the vertebrae and relieve pressure on spinal nerve roots. These adjustments are typically safe and effective and are a good drug free treatment option, but it's important to discuss any concerns you have with your chiropractor beforehand.
- **Flexion-distraction technique:** A specific chiropractic technique that gently stretches the spine to create space between the vertebrae and reduce pressure on the herniated disc.
- **Pelvic blocking techniques:** Stabilizing the pelvis to promote proper spinal alignment and reduce pain.
- **Massage therapy:** Loosening tight muscles and improving circulation to relieve pain through manual therapy. A [massage therapist](/massage-therapy/) may treat herniated discs during chiropractic visits.
- **Stretching and exercise:** [Tailored exercises](/therapeutic-exercises/) to strengthen core muscles and improve flexibility, preventing future adverse events and disc issues.

### Re-Evaluate and Adjust Treatment Plan Accordingly

Throughout your treatment, your chiropractor will monitor your progress and adjust the plan as needed. This ensures that you are receiving the most effective care possible and allows for adjustments based on your response to treatment and other symptoms that may arise.

Remember, chiropractic care is a collaborative process. Open communication with your chiropractor is key to achieving optimal results.

By following these steps, chiropractic treatment can help you manage pain, improve mobility, and promote healing if you have a herniated disc. While it's not a cure-all, it can be a valuable tool in your recovery journey.

## Spinal Manipulation and Other Treatment Options

Spinal manipulation, often associated with chiropractic care, involves controlled, targeted adjustments to the spine. While research doesn't definitively prove its effectiveness in "fixing" a herniated disc, it can often:

**Reduce pain and inflammation:** By taking pressure off irritated nerves.

**Improve mobility and range of motion:** By restoring proper spinal alignment.

**Prevent further disc damage:** By promoting stability and reducing strain on the discs.

Other chiropractic techniques like flexion-distraction and pelvic blocking can also offer significant pain relief and support healing.

## Chiropractic Therapy vs. Surgery for Herniated Disc

Chiropractic care is a conservative treatment option often recommended before considering spinal surgery. However, some rare cases may require early surgical intervention, especially if:

- You have severe neurological symptoms like muscle weakness or loss of bowel or bladder control.
- Conservative treatments haven't provided significant relief within a reasonable timeframe.
- The herniation is causing progressive nerve damage.

Remember, your chiropractor will work closely with your primary medical care physician or neurologist to determine the best course of treatment based on your unique situation.

## What to Know About Chiropractic Care

While traditional [chiropractic adjustments](https://www.mayoclinic.org/tests-procedures/chiropractic-adjustment/about/pac-20393513) often steal the spotlight, several other techniques can significantly contribute to managing herniated disc pain. Let's delve into two such techniques, flexion-distraction and pelvic blocking, and explore where you can find top-notch chiropractic care for your herniated disc.

### Flexion-distraction technique

This gentle chiropractic technique involves lying on a specialized table that stretches the spine while applying a slight distraction force. This creates space between the vertebrae, potentially allowing the herniated disc material to retract and reducing pressure on nerve roots.

### Pelvic blocking techniques

These techniques utilize wedges or pads placed under specific points of the pelvis to stabilize it and improve spinal alignment. This can indirectly relieve pressure on the discs and reduce pain.

## FAQ: Chiropractor Herniated Disc

Let's address some common questions you might have about chiropractic care and herniated discs:

### Does chiropractic adjustment work in treating herniated discs?

Research suggests that chiropractic adjustments can effectively reduce pain and improve function in many patients with herniated discs. However, it's not a guaranteed cure and may not work for everyone.

### Are there any side effects from chiropractic treatments for herniated discs?

Most chiropractic treatments are considered safe when performed by a qualified professional. However, some mild side effects like temporary soreness or stiffness may occur after adjustments.

### Can chiropractic treatment and surgery be used together for herniated disc?

Yes, in some cases, chiropractic care can be used alongside surgery to manage herniated disc symptoms and promote healing. Your doctor and chiropractor will work together to determine the best approach for your individual needs.

### Can a chiropractor fix a herniated disc?

No, chiropractic adjustments cannot "fix" a herniated disc in the sense of repairing the damaged tissue. However, they can significantly reduce pain, improve mobility, and prevent further disc damage. Surgery is the only way to physically repair a herniated disc, but it should only be considered after conservative treatments have been exhausted.

### Can chiropractor make herniated disc or bulging disc worse?

While rare, improper chiropractic manipulations, especially in cases of severe disc herniation or underlying instability, could potentially worsen symptoms. However, when performed by a qualified professional with the right expertise, the risk of such complications is minimal.
