The spine coordinates your body’s major functions, including moving your arms and legs, walking, standing, and performing daily tasks. When spinal misalignment and other back problems occur, it can lead to serious difficulties with mobility, range of motion, balance, and coordination.

Chiropractic care is a viable option for those who have chronic back pain and who want a non-surgical procedure. Chiropractors’ main goal is to ensure the spine is aligned and in its correct position.

You need to immediately seek a chiropractor if you have experienced these signs and symptoms of spine misalignment.

- Tingling sensation in the hands and feet
- Numbness
- Chronic headache
- Pain in the hips
- Posture problems
- Stiffness
- Lower back pain
- Extreme fatigue
- Neck pain
- Abnormalities when walking
- Unexpected and frequent illness

It is a constant struggle when your spine is misaligned. Aside from being unable to function properly, you suffer from reduced overall quality of life. Do not delay treating your back problems any longer. You should see a chiropractor if spinal misalignment causes you lasting pain.
