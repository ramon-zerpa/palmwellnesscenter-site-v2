FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a referral to see a primary care provider?

Often no referral is required. Some insurance and Medicare plans may request one from your existing provider - our team can verify your coverage and coordinate it.

What should I bring to my first appointment?

A valid ID, your insurance card, a current medication list, and any relevant medical records. If you have recent labs or imaging, we can review them during your visit.

Do you accept my insurance?

We accept most major insurance plans, including Medicare. Call (813) 443-5370 to verify your specific coverage before your visit.

Can I see my provider by telemedicine?

Yes - telemedicine visits are available throughout Florida for follow-ups, medication management, lab review, and chronic care monitoring.

How long are appointments?

Initial primary care visits are typically 60 minutes and follow-ups 30-45 minutes, so your provider has time to listen and explain.

What if I need specialist care?

Your provider coordinates referrals to trusted specialists across Tampa Bay and ensures continuity of care.
